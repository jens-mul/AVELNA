# AVELNA — First Production Proof

## What was implemented

The founders' already approved Dutch bathroom still is transferred into the **real Higgsfield private media project**. The first motion job uses the exact prompt in [`prompts/hero-approach-v1.md`](../prompts/hero-approach-v1.md).

The objective is to evaluate whether a premium camera push-in preserves the static room, mat silhouette, floor contact and architectural geometry. This is a **technical creative proof**, not a commercial product video or proof of water absorption.

A provider-side validation rejected 3:4. The supported 9:16 version was re-quoted at 5 credits and submitted **once**. Do not claim a completed video, successful quality check, or paid ad until status and footage have actually been read back.

## Source hierarchy and trust

1. Product reality: physical sample, exact SKU, dimensions, independently reviewable product image rights.
2. Product concept: all generated stills with nonverified geometry remain concept_only.
3. Architecture: the founder approved the Dutch bathroom's environment art direction.
4. Motion: founder authorized this one 5-credit image-to-video test.
5. Sales and advertising: a separate authorization requiring the real SKU and claims verification.
