# AVELNA research
Use `docs/competitors.md`, `research/sources.md` and `prompts/competitor-analyst.md`.
- Open competitor pages, Meta Ad Library and official sources lawfully, with citations, time and country.
- Record only really seen ad IDs and status. Differentiate product pages from actual ads.
- Max six brands and five unique concepts each for first sweep; incremental diff twice weekly, not a perpetual paid crawler.
- Three hypotheses maximum; one creative brief aligned to AVELNA Quiet Architecture.
- No personal data copying, scraping around blocks, supplier outreach, Vaultwarden or paid provider calls.
