# First creative and commercial hypotheses

1. **Editorial transformation first:** a rigid Bath Stone visually integrated into a realistic calm bathroom justifies a premium perceived price better than an isolated catalogue render. Source: Dorai product presentation; validation is AVELNA's *own* order economics, not a competitor ad duration.
2. **Material questions reduce conversion:** adding honest material, grip, care and measured dimensions near checkout may reduce uncertainty. Source: current stone-mat category presentations. Measure FAQ engagement/checkout abandonment without treating engagement as acquisition.
3. **One trusted master reduces waste:** generating a single approved hero once, with deterministic crops and typography, reduces per-usable-asset cost and image drift compared to regenerating every creative. Measure actual credits and rejection counts.
4. **Single-SKU price testing:** €49.95 incl. shipping is an initial hypothesis. If landed cost makes target contribution unachievable, pause spending instead of promising a profitable ROAS.

Do not launch four simultaneously under €60. First test: one editorial concept (motion + static) or no-spend waitlist only when not ready to sell. Only actual paid orders plus post-refund contribution establish customer acquisition.
